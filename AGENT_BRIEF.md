# Agent Brief — Step 3

## Product description

A calm, three-screen mock-up for learning one historical or cultural fact at a time (sourced from Wikipedia). It is intentionally **not** a feed: no infinite scroll, no endless cards. Each visit is meant to feel like a short, deliberate act of becoming more informed.

## Step 1 (carry over)

Lead with value.

- **Fundamental value:** Edification. The person feels proud of the knowledge they gained and that they used their phone wisely.
- **Need:** They want to be informed and able to talk about interesting subjects, but they do not want that to mean only current events. News and habitual phone use leave them with fleeting updates instead of lasting understanding of people, places, and how the world came to be.
- **Persona:** A maturing adult seeking knowledge; uses a phone frequently; strives to use phone time in more self-edifying ways.
- **Primary capability:** Learn something clear about an interesting person, place, or event (past or present) in one short sitting.

## Affordance sentence (dominant on landing)

**Use your phone to edify yourself, one fact at a time.**

This sentence must be the first thing a first-time user encounters. Nothing on the landing screen may compete with it or with the primary action that follows from it.

## Three screens (Step 2)

| Screen | Name | One job | Why it earned a slot | Design question it answers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Landing | Signal edification + the one-fact capability | First contact must communicate value and the main action | Does the landing communicate value and capability at a glance? |
| 2 | Fact | Deliver one Wikipedia-sourced fact and let them go deeper or get another | Shows the product working—the capability in action | Is one fact enough to feel edifying without inviting scroll addiction? |
| 3 | Interests | Let them choose topics that shape what they learn next | Makes “variety of people, places, events” concrete without becoming settings theater | Do topic choices feel like control over learning, not configuration busywork? |

### Screen details

**Screen 1 — Landing**
- Dominant text: affordance sentence above
- Primary button (preferred): Learn something new!
- Secondary button: Customize my interests
- Obvious path into the capability; home is this screen

**Screen 2 — Fact**
- Body: one random Wikipedia excerpt (respect selected interests when set)
- Actions: Read more! (open source article), Next fact!, Customize my interests
- Navigation back to landing

**Screen 3 — Customize my interests**
- Prompt: Select topics that interest you
- Selectable subject buttons
- Button: Learn something new!
- Navigation back to landing

## Preferences (tone / content / UX)

- Anti-brainrot: discrete facts, intentional “next,” no infinite scroll or feed layout
- Historical / cultural knowledge over news-cycle urgency
- Calm, uplifting, edifying tone—not gamified streaks or dopamine chrome
- Three screens only; look like one product; Gestalt grouping of related actions/content
- Keep UI simple enough for a class mock-up—consistent, not a design system
- Mobile-friendly; works in a browser without login
