## STEP 6

### Need

Q: How do you use your phone to edify yourself now, and what is annoying about it?

A: I scroll through my chrome suggested articles, the New York Times, and Reddit to try to stay informed. What’s annoying is I feel the constantly scrolling actually keeps me trapped. Fully news sources like NYT don’t really uplift me and make me feel worse about the state of the world and apps like reddit have too many distractions and dopamine hits that I feel I waste too much of my time on them, despite trying to better myself.

### Value

Q: What kind of app would you want to use to avoid these frustrations you feel when you are trying to stay informed?

A: The biggest problem with predatory apps is the dopamine hits and encouragement to scroll. I’d like something that I still feel is interesting but doesn’t suck my time. I also want to learn more about the world, not just current events and so an app that focuses on broader education would help me stay more optimistic.

### Persona

Q: Do you know anyone else that struggles with these same frustrations?

A: I think that most people feel dissatisfaction in the way they use their devices, or rather how their predatory apps use them, but there is probably a range from people who feel the dissatisfaction but don’t care to people actively looking for replacement apps that help edify their life. I also have some friends who really don’t care about the amount of time they spend scrolling. 

### Capability

Q: Explore this app and tell me what you think this app does and how it can help you?

A: This looks like it gives me a random fact sourced from wikipedia. It is pretty simple, only allowing small control over the type of fact returned and a screen to pull a random fact. I think this could help me to learn more but it needs some work to truly give me value. It’s a good start! And I’ve already learned more on it than I would have on a social media or dedicated news app
